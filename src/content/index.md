## Introduction

The {figures.year} mid-year estimate (MYE) is the latest official estimate of the population for local authorities in England and Wales. Estimates are produced annually by the Office for National Statistics (ONS).

This site describes London's population now and how it has changed since 2011. It covers:

- the key points, the change in population and how the {figures.year} estimate compares with previous mid-year estimates
- the components of change: births, deaths and migration
- the age structure of the population
